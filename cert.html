Certification of trust

Let’s write your certification of trust

A Certification of Trust proves your living trust exists and shows your authority as Trustee — without handing over the entire trust document. Banks, brokerages, and title companies often ask for one before they’ll open an account or transfer property into your trust’s name. Answer a few plain questions and watch it take shape. Your answers stay in this browser.

Need something stronger, sworn under oath? Write an Affidavit of Trustee instead. Moving everyday belongings into your trust? Sign a General Assignment of Personal Property. Don’t have a living trust yet? Write your living trust first, or the joint version if you’re creating one with a spouse or partner.
