NOTES ON YOUR IMPORTANT CONTACTS LIST
======================================

WHAT THIS IS
There was no source document for this one -- you asked for a simple contacts list (Name,
Relationship, Telephone, Email) and said to come up with the rest. It's a plain printable
reference: the people someone might need to call while handling your affairs. It isn't a legal
document, so there's nothing to sign, no state question, and no "SAMPLE - NOT FOR SIGNING"
watermark like every other document on this site -- the watermark exists to flag unverified legal
language, and there's no legal language here to flag.

WHAT THE QUESTIONS COVER
Who the list is prepared for, then as many contacts as you want to add -- each with a name
(the only required field), relationship, phone, and email. The "Add another contact" placeholders
cycle through a few commonly forgotten roles (estate attorney, executor, primary doctor, a family
member, financial advisor) just as prompts -- they don't have to be used and nothing is pre-filled.
The signing-instructions panel on the review screen also lists roles people often forget to
include: your executor, successor trustee, and any agents named in your power of attorney or
health care directive; your accountant, insurance agent; your doctor and any specialists.

A REAL BUG THIS CAUGHT
Building a 4-field row (the first time this site needed more than two inputs per row) surfaced a
genuine bug in the shared input-sync code in js/will.js: the listener that copies what someone
types into the answers object only recognized `input[type="text"]`, `input[type="date"]`, and a
couple of others -- it didn't recognize `type="tel"` or `type="email"`, so anything typed into a
phone or email field displayed on screen but was silently never saved. Fixed by adding both types
to that listener's selector list. This was a live bug the whole time, just never triggered before
because no other document on the site used those input types -- now that it's fixed, any future
phone or email field anywhere on the site will work correctly too.

HOW TO EDIT
File: will/contacts-template.js   the document itself (settings + the full text)
File: will/contacts-signing.js    the "what to do with this list" panel shown after review
File: css/will.css                the new ".rowitem.grid4" rule (a 2x2 layout for the 4-field
                                  contact rows) lives here, added alongside the existing row styles
The notes at the top of each file explain the codes used ([[if x]], [[each contacts]], and so on).
