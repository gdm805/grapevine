NOTES ON YOUR DRAFT WILL  (what I kept, changed and noticed)
=============================================================

The will text in will/will-template.js is your own draft ("last will - unformatted"), word for word,
with names and choices turned into blanks and options. I compared the generated will against your
original paragraph by paragraph. These are the only differences:

CHANGED
1. Section 6.03, last sentence. Your draft says the share is "distributed under Article Six", which is
   the article the sentence is already in. I pointed it to the Remote Contingent Distribution section
   (Section 6.04 in a will like yours). Please confirm that is what you meant.
2. Section 4.07 (Order of Distribution). Your draft mentions "Specific Gifts", but your will has none.
   The phrase now appears only when the person has made a specific gift.
3. Section 8.02. Your draft breaks one sentence across two paragraphs. I joined them.
4. Section 8.03, last paragraph (community property). It now appears only when the person is married AND lives
   in a community property state (Arizona, California, Idaho, Louisiana, Nevada, New Mexico, Texas, Washington,
   Wisconsin, per your list). California says "community or quasi-community property"; the others say
   "community property". The list is in will/states.js. Your draft showed it to every married person.
5. Execution. Your draft has the date (2026-09-10) and county typed in, and the county differs from
   the county of residence. Those are now blank lines the person fills in by hand when signing.
   "We were both present" becomes "all present" in a state that needs three witnesses (Vermont).
6. A stray line "Last Will and Testament of ... Page 12 of 14" was sitting inside Section 8.06.
   I removed it. A running footer with page numbers is added when the will is printed.
7. Section numbers are automatic. Your draft skipped 1.06, 6.04 and 7.10 because optional sections
   were left out. They now close up on their own, and cross-references such as "Article Six" follow.

ADDED (your draft did not have them)
8. Guardian of Minor Children. Shown when the person has a child under 18. Replace my wording

9. Specific Gifts article. Shown when the person adds a gift. Replace my wording with yours.
   (If your missing Section 6.04 was something else, such as a trust for young beneficiaries,

   Because your Section 5.13 already lets the Personal Representative handle property for a minor,

THINGS I NOTICED, LEFT AS YOU WROTE THEM
10. Defined but never used: "Routine Administrative Acts" (Section 7.09) and "Surviving Spouse"

13. The attestation has no statements about capacity or duress, and there is no notary page. I set
    every state to "no self-proving affidavit". The affidavit page is still in the template if you need it.

THE QUESTIONS
- "Personal Representative" is used throughout, and the question explains that some states say executor.
- Residuary: a person can start from "all to my spouse", "equally to my children" (shares add up to
  exactly 100%, with any extra cent going to the first child), or "specific people", then adjust.
  For each one they pick what happens if that person does not survive them, using your options
  (descendants, a named person, a charity, or the others share it). Your Section 6.05 covers the rest.
