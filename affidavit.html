Affidavit of trustee

Let’s write your affidavit of trustee

An Affidavit of Trustee is a sworn statement — not just a signed one — confirming your identity, status, and authority as Trustee. It's useful for a specific pending transaction, or when a recipient wants more than a Certification of Trust provides. Answer a few plain questions and watch it take shape. Your answers stay in this browser.

Just need to prove your trust exists and your general authority? A Certification of Trust is usually easier to get accepted. Moving everyday belongings into your trust? Sign a General Assignment of Personal Property. Don’t have a living trust yet? Write your living trust first.
