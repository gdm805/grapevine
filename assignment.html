Assignment of personal property

Let’s assign your personal property to your trust

A General Assignment of Personal Property to Trust is a backstop — one signed document covering the everyday belongings that don’t have a title or account to change: furniture, jewelry, tools, and the like. No state requires a witness or notary for it. Answer a few plain questions and watch it take shape. Your answers stay in this browser.

This only covers property without its own title or account — real estate, vehicles, and financial accounts still need to be retitled directly. Don’t have a living trust yet? Write your living trust first. Need to prove your trust exists to a bank or title company? Create a Certification of Trust.
