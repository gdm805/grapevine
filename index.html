    Plain language

    Legal words are exact. Understanding them shouldn’t require a law degree.

    Every question you answer is in plain English — here’s how one of those answers becomes real legal wording. Flip the switch to compare.

    Last Will and Testament

    Article II · Guardian

    Should it become necessary for a guardian of the person of any minor child of mine to be appointed, I hereby nominate and appoint my sister, Lucía Alvarez, to serve as such guardian, and should she fail or cease to act for any reason, I nominate my friend, Priya Nair, as successor guardian.

    If I can’t look after my children, I want my sister, Lucía Alvarez, to raise them. If she can’t, I choose my friend, Priya Nair.

    Same meaning, different words.
