Let’s write your HIPAA authorization

This lets the people you choose — your health care agent, family, or anyone else — get your medical records and talk to your doctors, even before any power of attorney or health care directive kicks in. No witness or notary is required. The exact wording is different in every state. Answer a few plain questions and watch it take shape. Your answers stay in this browser.
