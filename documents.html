Not sure what you need? Answer 6 quick questions

Wills

  • Last Will and TestamentSay who receives what you own, who carries out your wishes, and who raises your children.
  • Pour-Over WillThe will that goes with a living trust: it sends anything left outside the trust into it.

Living trust

  • Revocable Living TrustHold your home and savings in a trust so they pass to your family privately, without probate.
  • Joint Revocable Living TrustOne shared trust for a married couple or domestic partners.

Trust paperwork

For a living trust you already have, whether you made it here or elsewhere.

  • Schedule A (Trust Property)The list of property in your trust, as its own page you can update whenever your property changes.
  • Certification of TrustShows a bank or title company that your trust exists and you can act for it, without handing over the whole trust.
  • Affidavit of TrusteeA sworn statement of who you are and your authority as trustee, for a transaction or general use.
  • General Assignment of Personal PropertyMoves your everyday belongings into your trust without a separate title change for each item.

Money and health care

  • Durable Power of AttorneyChoose someone you trust to pay bills and handle your money and paperwork if you can’t.
  • Health Care DirectiveName who makes medical decisions for you and record your wishes, in your state’s own wording.
  • Dementia Care PreferencesYour wishes for care if you ever develop dementia or memory loss.
  • HIPAA AuthorizationLets the people you choose get your medical information from doctors and hospitals.

For your family

  • Final WishesYour wishes for burial or cremation and a funeral or memorial, and who carries them out.
  • Important ContactsA printable list of the people your family or executor may need to reach.
